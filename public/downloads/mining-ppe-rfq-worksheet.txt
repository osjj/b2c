Important distinctions:
- Bump caps do not replace safety helmets for falling-object protection.
- A helmet lamp mount is not lamp approval or assembled-system certification.
- Antistatic footwear is not electrical insulating footwear.
- Routine dust respirators do not replace mine-specific escape equipment.
- Equipment requirements depend on the mine, task and jurisdiction.
