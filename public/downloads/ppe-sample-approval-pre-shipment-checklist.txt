LIMITS
This template does not certify PPE, establish document authenticity by itself,
prove all units' performance, or replace hazard assessment, required testing,
respirator program duties, training, user inspection, maintenance or replacement.
Agree inspection and sampling with the responsible buyer and qualified inspector;
no universal AQL or sample size is prescribed. Apply relevant destination and
workplace requirements. Store completed records with appropriate access controls.
