# Construction PPE SEO Cluster Map

## Hub Rule

The construction PPE cluster has one broad commercial hub:

| URL | Primary Keyword | Secondary Keywords | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/solutions/construction-site-ppe-solution` | construction site PPE | construction PPE supplier, construction safety equipment, PPE for construction workers, bulk construction PPE, construction PPE solution | Commercial hub for broad construction PPE searches and quote intent |

All blog spokes should support this hub. Blog pages can mention construction PPE naturally, but they should not target broad commercial phrases such as `construction PPE supplier`, `construction safety equipment supplier`, or `construction PPE solution` as their primary keyword.

## Internal Linking Rules

- Hub links to spokes with exact descriptive anchors, for example `construction PPE checklist` or `OSHA PPE requirements for construction`.
- Spokes link back to the hub or a more specific solution page with commercial but differentiated anchors.
- Category and product links should match the spoke topic instead of repeating the same site-wide PPE bundle.
- New construction blog topics must be checked against this map before drafting. If the primary keyword already exists here, update the existing page instead of creating a competing page.

## Metadata Rules

- Hub metadata may use broad commercial phrases: `construction PPE supplier`, `construction safety equipment`, `bulk construction PPE`, `construction PPE solution`.
- Blog metadata should use precise informational or task-based phrases.
- Procurement spokes may use `bulk` language, but should avoid claiming the generic supplier keyword unless the page is intended to replace the hub.
